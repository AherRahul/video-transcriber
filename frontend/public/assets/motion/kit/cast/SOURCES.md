# The cast - sources

The people are our own drawings: the player's rig (frontend/src/motion/player/kit/toon.ts) draws them from the
specs in cast.json (hair, accessories and colours per person; one figure and outfit for everyone). No outside
art is used.

Before 2026-11-18 the cast was Open Peeps (CC0) art; the founder replaced it (the body changed between poses and
the faces were too plain), so those files and their build script were removed.
